A little clarity

Before you get started.

Can I try creating a profile?

Yes. Choose a photo and fill in the form to see a local preview. Nothing is uploaded or saved. Closing or reloading this page does not create a member account.

Will my date of birth be shown publicly?

The proposed public profile shows age, not your exact date of birth. This preview does not store either value.

How does payment approval work?

The planned process is a bank transfer followed by a slip sent through WhatsApp. The administrator confirms the actual bank payment before issuing a code. This preview cannot accept or approve payments.

Are these real members?

No. All directory cards are clearly marked illustrative samples.
