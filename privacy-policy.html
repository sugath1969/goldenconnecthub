Privacy Policy

Draft for review. This policy describes GoldenConnect's current private-profile testing stage. Public profile publishing and payments are not currently enabled.

1. Who operates GoldenConnect

GoldenConnect is operated personally by Sugath Wijesinghe. It is a companionship and social-connection service intended for people aged 40 and above in Sri Lanka.

For privacy questions and requests, contact support.goldenconnect@gmail.com .

2. Information you provide

When you register, we collect your email address and use an authentication service to manage your password and account sign-in.

If you create a profile, the information you submit may include your name, age, gender, location, occupation, tagline, personal introduction, companionship preferences, and an uploaded photograph.

If you contact us, we also receive the information you include in your message. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment-card details, or unnecessary identity documents.

3. How information is used

We use information to:

  • Create and manage accounts and sign-in sessions.
  • Save and display your profile to you.
  • Support email confirmation and password recovery.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Investigate technical problems, misuse, and security concerns.

4. Profile visibility

During the current testing stage, saving a profile does not publish it in a public member directory. Public publishing and member contact access are not enabled.

If public publishing or member-to-member access is introduced, we will explain the visibility rules and update this policy before enabling those features.

Private does not mean that information is inaccessible to the service operator or the providers needed to operate the service.

5. Service providers and information sharing

GoldenConnect uses Supabase for account authentication, profile data, and photograph storage; Vercel for website hosting; and Cloudflare for domain-related infrastructure. These providers process information as needed to supply their services.

Hosting and authentication services may process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser information, request details, and security logs.

Information handled by service providers may be processed outside Sri Lanka. Their own privacy terms also apply to their processing.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

6. Browser storage

Account sign-in uses browser storage to maintain your session, including when you refresh the page. Signing out ends the session in that browser. Clearing browser storage may also sign you out.

7. Payments

GoldenConnect does not currently accept membership payments through this website. Before payments are enabled, we will update this policy to identify the payment provider and explain the payment-related information we receive.

8. Retention and deletion requests

We retain account and profile information while it is needed to provide and administer your account.

To request deletion of your GoldenConnect account, profile information, and uploaded photograph, email support.goldenconnect@gmail.com . We may ask you to verify that you own the account before processing your request. We aim to process verified deletion requests within 14 days after verification.

Deletion requests are handled manually. This timeframe does not mean that every backup or service-provider log is erased immediately. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations, security investigations, or resolving disputes.

9. Access and corrections

You can review and edit the available profile fields while signed in. For other requests about your personal information, contact the support email above. We may verify account ownership before providing information or making changes.

10. Security

We use account authentication and access restrictions to help protect information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. Access-security testing is still in progress during this development stage.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the service or its data-handling practices change. A publication or revision date will be added when this draft is approved for publication.
