GoldenConnect Membership Terms

Effective date: 9 November 2026

1. Agreement before payment

Before making a payment, you must read and agree to these membership terms. By making a payment, you confirm that you accept these terms.

2. Membership eligibility

Paid membership is available to people aged 40 or over. Once your payment is confirmed, you can view all eligible member profiles automatically.

3. Membership fee and payment

The membership fee is LKR 4,000 for one month. Payment is made through PayHere Lite. Membership begins only after GoldenConnect receives confirmation of payment.

4. Membership period

Membership lasts for one month from the payment confirmation date.

5. Renewal

There is no automatic renewal. After one month, you must make a new payment to continue membership.

6. Membership end

After the one-month membership period ends, your profile will be hidden from the public website. It will remain hidden until a new payment is confirmed.

7. Cancellation

You can cancel your membership at any time. There is no refund for the remaining membership period. Your profile will be hidden when you cancel.

8. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account at any time. GoldenConnect will remove your profile and personal details from the website. Membership ends when the account is deleted. There is no refund for the remaining membership period.

9. Membership benefits

Paid members can view eligible profiles and contact other members directly through GoldenConnect. Contact details are shared only when both members choose to share them.

10. Profile visibility

Once you complete the required profile details, your profile will be visible automatically to eligible paid members. GoldenConnect may hide or remove a profile if it breaks these terms or GoldenConnect’s safety rules.

11. Respectful conduct

Members must treat other members with respect. You may send messages and share contact details only when both members agree.

12. Safety and prohibited conduct

GoldenConnect strictly prohibits harassment, abuse, threats, discrimination, fake profiles, impersonation, illegal activity, and sharing another person’s private information without permission. GoldenConnect may hide, restrict, suspend, or remove any account or profile that breaks these rules.

13. Privacy and personal information

GoldenConnect uses personal information only to provide the membership service, protect members, and comply with legal requirements.

14. Refund policy

  • Duplicate payments will be refunded.
  • If an unresolved technical problem prevents GoldenConnect from providing the membership service, a full or proportionate refund may be given.
  • There is no refund for a change of mind or for unused membership benefits.
  • Refund requests must be made within 14 days of payment.
  • GoldenConnect will make a decision within 14 days of receiving the necessary information.
  • Bank or card refunds may take additional time, depending on the payment provider.

15. Payment-activation problems

If your membership does not become active after payment, email support.goldenconnect@gmail.com with:

  • Your GoldenConnect email address
  • The payment date and time
  • Your PayHere reference number or payment receipt

GoldenConnect will respond within 3 business days.

16. Disputes and governing law

If you have a problem, please contact GoldenConnect support first. These terms are governed by the laws of Sri Lanka.

17. Changes to these terms

GoldenConnect may change these terms from time to time. Any changes will be published on this website before they take effect. If you continue using GoldenConnect after the changes take effect, you agree to the updated terms.

18. Contact us

For questions or support, contact GoldenConnect at support.goldenconnect@gmail.com.
